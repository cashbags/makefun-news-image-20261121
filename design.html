Peru — Lead story

Quake shakes Peru, felt in Lima

A magnitude 6.8 earthquake strikes the Andes of Junín at 19:09 local time on Friday — 00:09 UTC on Saturday — and sends Lima residents into the streets and lawmakers out of Congress.

MAGNITUDE 6.8, depth 129 km (IGP; USGS: 108 km). NO CASUALTIES or major damage reported.

Poland — Airspace

A Russian missile falls on Poland

A Kh-101 cruise missile crashes near Nowy Lipowiec, about 50 km from the Ukrainian border, during one of Russia’s heaviest air attacks on Ukraine. PM Tusk calls it a deliberate provocation; NATO’s Rutte is due in Warsaw on Saturday.

Markets — Correction

Wall Street slides into correction

Friday’s rout puts the S&P 500 −3.70% and the Dow −1.93% into correction, and the Nasdaq −5.28% wipes out its gain for the year. Nvidia falls 13.8% as the SEC examines possible accounting issues.

Antalya — Climate

COP31 misses its deadline

The UN climate summit runs into overtime after its Friday deadline passes with no deal. The latest draft never mentions fossil fuels.

The Gulf — Blockade

Iran masses tankers at Kharg

Satellite data puts 14+ Iranian-flagged tankers at Kharg Island, most with trackers off, as Tehran prepares a convoy to challenge the blockade.

Riyadh — Escalation

Missiles down over Riyadh

Saudi defences intercept three ballistic missiles fired at Riyadh in the early hours of Saturday, the third night of alerts in the capital.
